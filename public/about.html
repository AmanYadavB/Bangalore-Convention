What this is

A gathering of the fellowship of Alcoholics Anonymous, hosted in Bangalore over three days in July 2027. Members travel from across India and beyond to share experience, strength and hope — in meetings, in workshops, and in the ordinary conversations that happen between them.

It is organised by a volunteer committee, run not for profit, and funded entirely by the registrations of the people who attend.

Who it is for

Anyone with a desire to stop drinking is welcome, as are the members of Al-Anon and other fellowships where the convention programme includes them. You do not need an invitation and you do not need to belong to a particular group or region.

Committee · not published

What happens over the three days

Three days of meetings, workshops and fellowship, opening on the Friday morning and closing on the Sunday.

Committee · not published
The shape of the programme — how many main sessions, the workshop format, the marathon meetings, the countdown, and anything running alongside. The full schedule is not expected before Q4 2026, but a paragraph describing the shape of the weekend can be written now, and is what most people read this page for.

Anonymity

Anonymity is the spiritual foundation of the fellowship, and this convention is run accordingly. We never publish attendee names or lists. We keep our public name, our emails and our notifications neutral, so nothing appearing on your phone or in your inbox discloses anything about you to someone nearby.

Photography at the convention is restricted, and no photograph identifying a member is published without their written consent. How we handle the information you give us is set out in full in our Privacy Policy.

Where the money goes

The convention is not a fundraiser. Registration covers the cost of hosting it — venue, meals, accommodation where included, materials and the running of this website. There are no salaries and no commercial sponsors. Prices for each category are in the Payment Terms.

Getting to Bangalore

✈️

By air

Kempegowda International (BLR), about 35 km north of the centre. Airport buses, app taxis and the prepaid counter run through the night — allow 60 to 90 minutes, longer in the evening peak.

🚆

By train

KSR Bengaluru City (SBC), Yesvantpur Junction (YPR) and Bengaluru Cantonment (BNC). SBC is central and connected to the Namma Metro network.

🚌

By road

Long-distance buses run from across Karnataka and the neighbouring states, arriving mainly at the Kempegowda bus station opposite SBC.

Getting around the city

  • Namma Metro — the fastest way across the centre, and the cheapest. Buy a smart card or a QR ticket at any station.
  • App taxis and autos — widely available; auto-rickshaws are often quicker than a car over short distances.
  • Buses — BMTC covers the whole city, though routes take some learning if you are visiting for the first time.

Where you will stay

Accommodation is part of your registration if you choose a category that includes it — single, twin or triple sharing. If you register in a No Stay category, you are arranging your own accommodation and the convention does not hold rooms for you. The categories are in the Payment Terms.

Committee · not published
Check-in and check-out times, whether rooms are available the night before the convention opens, and how sharing partners are allocated for twin and triple categories.

July in Bangalore

July is the south-west monsoon. Expect mild temperatures — roughly 20 to 28 °C — with heavy showers that come and go rather than rain all day. Bring an umbrella or a light rain jacket, something warm for the evenings, and comfortable shoes that cope with wet pavements.

Coming from outside India

Delegates travelling from abroad are responsible for their own visas. If you need a letter of invitation for a visa application, write to support@biaac.com well ahead of your appointment — these take time to prepare and we cannot promise a same-week turnaround.

Frequently Asked Questions

The ten things people actually write in and ask. Every answer has its own link — open one and copy the address to send it to someone.

1. What is this convention?

The Bangalore International AA Convention 2027 — three days of fellowship, meetings and workshops in Bangalore from 9 to 11 July 2027, organised by a volunteer committee and run not for profit. There is more at the top of this page.

2. Who can come?

Anyone with a desire to stop drinking. You do not need an invitation, and you do not need to belong to a particular group or region.

Committee · not published
The answer for family, friends and Al-Anon members. Whatever is decided in Who it is for above belongs here too, in one sentence.
3. When and where exactly?

Friday 9 July to Sunday 11 July 2027, opening at 9:00 AM IST on the Friday, at RG Royal Hotel in Bangalore — full address under Venue & Travel. If you are booking travel, book to Bangalore.

4. What does it cost, and what is included?

Seven categories, from ₹2,000 for a single no-stay day to ₹16,000 for a single room with stay. The four No Stay categories (Full Event, Friday/Saturday/Sunday Only) don't include accommodation; the three With Stay categories (Triple/Twin/Single Sharing) include accommodation and meals. The full table is in the Payment Terms.

5. When does registration open?

Registration and online payment go live together in the next release of this site. The committee has not yet fixed that date, and we would rather name no date than name one that moves. This page and the home page will both carry it the moment it is set.

6. How do I pay, and is it safe?

Payment is taken online through Razorpay, one of India's licensed payment gateways. Card, UPI and bank details are handled entirely by Razorpay and never reach this website's servers. A small transaction charge is added at checkout and shown to you before you confirm — the Payment Terms explain exactly how it is calculated.

7. Can I cancel and get my money back?

There is a written refund and cancellation policy, and it is the policy that applies rather than anything said in conversation. Read it on the Refund & Cancellation page before you pay.

8. Is accommodation included?

Only if you choose a category that includes it. The No Stay categories cover the convention itself and nothing else — you arrange your own room. The single, twin and triple sharing categories include accommodation and meals.

9. Will anyone know I attended?

Not from us. We never publish attendee names or lists, and we never share who has registered with anyone outside the organising committee. Our emails and notifications are deliberately worded so that nothing appearing on your phone discloses anything about you to someone standing nearby. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what we hold and for how long.

10. How do I reach a real person?

Write to support@biaac.com, or use the contact button at the bottom of any page. A member of the committee — not a robot — replies within 48 hours, usually sooner.
