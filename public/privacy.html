Privacy Policy

How the Bangalore Convention 2027 website collects, uses and stores your information. Last updated 2 August 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by the organising committee of the Bangalore Convention 2027, a not-for-profit fellowship gathering. We collect personal information only to register you for the event, take payment, and answer your questions. We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Anonymity

We understand that attendance at this gathering is a private matter. We deliberately keep our public-facing name, our messaging and our email subject lines neutral, so that a notification appearing on your phone or in your inbox does not disclose anything about you to someone nearby. We never publish attendee names or lists, and we never share who has registered with anyone outside the organising committee.

What we collect

When you register:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your city
  • Gender, if you choose to provide it
  • Your accommodation category and any notes you add

When you pay:

  • A payment reference and whether the payment succeeded. Card, UPI and bank details are handled entirely by our payment provider and never reach our servers.

When you use the chat assistant:

  • The messages you type, so the assistant can answer them
  • If you use voice mode, the audio of what you say, which is converted to text and then discarded

When you message us on WhatsApp:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the content of your messages
  • A short rolling history of the recent conversation, kept for 24 hours so the assistant can follow the thread, then deleted automatically

Automatically:

  • Standard technical request information such as approximate timing and error details, used only to keep the site working

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. The only data stored in your browser is your theme preference and your sign-in state.

Why we collect it

  • To register you and reserve your accommodation
  • To take and confirm your payment
  • To send you your registration confirmation
  • To answer your questions through the assistant or by email
  • To plan catering, rooms and logistics using overall totals

Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary with the service providers that make the site work. Each processes your data only to provide their service:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, storage and the AI assistant
  • Razorpay — payment processing
  • Meta (WhatsApp) — delivering WhatsApp messages, if you choose to use that channel
  • Google and Groq — additional AI providers used when the primary assistant is unavailable
  • Deepgram — speech processing for voice mode
  • MailChannels — sending email

We do not share your information with anyone else, except where we are required to by law.

How long we keep it

Registration and payment records are kept until the convention has finished and its accounts are closed, after which they are deleted. WhatsApp conversation history is deleted automatically after 24 hours. Operational logs are kept for a short period and then discarded.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you no longer want messages from us, tell us and we will stop. To opt out of WhatsApp messages specifically, reply STOP to any message we send you.

Security

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with our hosting provider. Access is limited to the organising committee members who need it. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we keep the amount of information we collect deliberately small.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Contact us

For any question about this policy, or to request access to or deletion of your information, email support@biaac.com.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
