How to build a page

Open the chat (bottom-right) and try things like:

  • “Build a schedule page with a colourful timeline for the three days.”
  • “Create a live stats page that shows registrations and expenses.”
  • “Redesign the schedule page with a dark theme.”

The assistant shows a preview and a Publish button before anything goes live.
