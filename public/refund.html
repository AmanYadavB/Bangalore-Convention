Refund & Cancellation

What happens if you cancel, if we cancel, or if a payment goes wrong. This written policy is what applies.

If you cancel

To cancel, write to support@biaac.com from the email address you registered with, quoting your registration reference. A cancellation takes effect from the time we receive that email, not from when you decided.

The transaction charge

The payment gateway keeps its processing fee even when a payment is refunded — it is not returned to the convention.

If the convention is cancelled or moved

If the committee cancels the convention, every registration is refunded in full.

Failed and duplicate payments

These are not cancellations and this policy's cut-off dates do not apply to them. If a payment failed but money left your account, or you were charged twice, write to us and we will return it in full. See Payment Terms for what to send us.

How to reach us

support@biaac.com — a member of the committee replies within 48 hours. Please write from your registered email address and quote your reference; it is the fastest way to find your record.
