The transaction charge

A payment gateway charge is added to your registration fee at checkout. It is the fee the gateway takes for processing the payment — 2% of the amount plus 18% GST on that fee — and it is passed on rather than absorbed, so that the whole of your registration fee reaches the convention.

You will always see the charge, and the total, on the payment screen before you confirm. As an illustration, on a ₹1,000 payment the charge is about ₹24, and you would be asked to approve about ₹1,024.

Committee · not published
Confirm the exact rate once the live Razorpay account is approved, and state here whether the transaction charge is returned on a refund. The gateway does not return its own fee, so somebody carries it — say who.

How payment is taken

Payments are processed by Razorpay, a payment gateway licensed in India. You can pay by UPI, debit card, credit card, net banking or wallet, depending on what Razorpay offers you at the time.

Your card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's own secure screens. They never pass through, and are never stored on, this website's servers. All we receive back is a payment reference and whether the payment succeeded.

When your registration is confirmed

Your place is confirmed when the payment succeeds — not when the form is submitted. A confirmation email with your registration reference is sent to the address you gave us. If you do not receive it within a few minutes, check your spam folder before writing to us, then write to us.

An unpaid registration does not hold a place, and unpaid entries are cleared automatically.

If something goes wrong

  • The payment failed. Nothing is taken. Try again, or use a different method.
  • Money left your account but you saw an error. Do not pay twice. Write to support@biaac.com with the date, the amount and any reference you were shown. Banks usually reverse an uncaptured payment on their own within a few working days; if it does not reverse, we will refund it.
  • You paid twice by accident. Tell us and we will refund the duplicate.

Receipts

Your confirmation email is your receipt and carries your registration reference. Keep it — it is the quickest way for us to find your record if you need to ask us anything.
